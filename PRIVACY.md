# Ever After privacy policy

*Last updated: 11 October 2026*

Ever After is a wedding-planning app for Windows. It runs entirely on your own computer. There are no Ever After accounts and no Ever After servers, and nothing you put into the app is sent to the developer.

## Your wedding plans

Everything you plan (guests, budget, suppliers, seating, ideas, notes and so on) is saved in a wedding file on your computer, in the folder you choose. If you keep that folder in OneDrive or another cloud folder to share it, your cloud provider stores it under their own terms. Ever After itself never uploads it anywhere.

Backups are kept in a Backups folder inside the wedding's own folder. Pictures you add to the ideas board are kept in its Ideas folder.

## Pinterest

You can bring Pinterest pins onto your ideas board in two ways.

**By link.** When you paste the link to a public Pinterest board or pin, Ever After fetches that public board's feed, or that pin's page, from pinterest.com, and saves the pictures to your ideas board on your computer. No sign-in is involved.

**By connecting your Pinterest account.** If you connect your account in Settings, Ever After uses your own Pinterest developer app and asks Pinterest for **read-only** access to:

- your account's basic details (to show which account is connected)
- your boards, including secret boards
- the pins on those boards

Ever After uses this access only to list your boards and copy the pins you choose onto your ideas board on your computer. It never creates, changes or deletes anything on Pinterest, and it doesn't store or share your Pinterest data anywhere except your own computer.

Your Pinterest app ID, app secret and sign-in are stored on your computer only, encrypted with Windows' own data protection, and are never saved in the wedding file. You can disconnect at any time in Settings → Connections, or remove Ever After's access in your Pinterest settings under [Security and logins](https://www.pinterest.com/settings/security).

## Spotify

If you connect Spotify, Ever After uses your own Spotify developer app to search for songs and, only when you ask, to make playlists of your wedding music in your account. It asks Spotify for permission to read your basic profile and to create and change playlists. Your sign-in is stored on your computer only, encrypted, and you can disconnect at any time in Settings → Connections.

## Updates

Ever After checks GitHub for new versions when it opens and every few hours, and downloads them from GitHub. This sends GitHub the usual details of a download request (such as your IP address), as with any download, but nothing about you or your wedding.

## Troubleshooting log

Ever After keeps a log on your computer to help work out what went wrong if something does. It stays on your computer, and is only shared if you copy it out yourself (Settings → Troubleshooting → Copy diagnostics).

## Children

Ever After isn't directed at children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here, with the date at the top.
